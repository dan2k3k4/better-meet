# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Better Meet ("the extension") is a browser extension for Google Meet. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

## What the extension accesses

- **Google Meet pages (`https://meet.google.com/*`)**: the extension reads and interacts with the Meet page in your browser to mute your mic and camera before joining, to detect when someone is waiting to join, and to show a timer. Nothing from the page is saved or sent anywhere.
- **Settings (`storage` permission)**: your on/off choices for each feature are saved with Chrome's `storage.sync`. If you're signed into Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs them between your devices. The extension's author never receives them.
- **Notifications (`notifications` permission)**: used only to show a local system notification when someone is waiting to join your meeting.

## What the extension does not do

- No servers: the extension never sends data to the author or to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising.
- No remote code: everything that runs is in the published package.
