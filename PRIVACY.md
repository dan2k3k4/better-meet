# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Better Meet ("the extension") is a browser extension for Google Meet. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

## What the extension accesses

- **Google Meet pages (`https://meet.google.com/*`)**: the extension reads and interacts with the Meet page in your browser to mute your mic and camera before joining, to detect when someone is waiting to join, to show a timer, and to show and toggle your mic status from the toolbar icon or a keyboard shortcut. Nothing from the page is saved or sent anywhere.
- **Settings (`storage` permission)**: your on/off choices for each feature are saved with Chrome's `storage.sync`. If you're signed into Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs them between your devices. The extension's author never receives them.
- **Notifications (`notifications` permission)**: used only to show local system notifications when someone is waiting to join your meeting, or when you leave the Meet tab with your mic on.

## What the extension does not do

- No servers: the extension never sends data to the author or to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising.
- No remote code: everything that runs is in the published package.
